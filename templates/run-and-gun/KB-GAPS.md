# KB gaps found building FIREBASE

Each line: what was asked, what the KB said, what was true, the evidence.
Tool answers are from c64-kb 0.29.0 (KB data 843) on the branch graph
`c64_rg`, 2026-09-26. Measured figures are VICE x64sc 3.10 (PAL C64C and
`-model ntsc`), from this starter's `make shot check` unless named.

1. **plan-budget, row_map_redraw.** Asked: `plan-budget` on the fingerprint. Said: row_map_redraw 13,304 cycles summed into every play frame (range 36,916-47,124, over the frame). True: the redraw runs on one frame in eight, the one YSCROLL wraps on, and the game runs no logic then. Measured: frames that run the logic take at most 3,049 cycles (PAL) and 3,071 (NTSC) with the IRQs; the redraw is 14,100 (PAL) and 14,317 (NTSC) on its own frame. The tool has no phase for "one frame in N" (play, transition, init only). (Known before this build; the Cost line itself was corrected from 14,673 on the branch.)
2. **game-briefing, proposals from brief words.** Asked: `game-briefing` with a run-and-gun brief. Said (this brief): `raycaster_grid_walls` ("walls"), `tile_map_render` ("map"), `relocated_code_block` ("run", "block"), `multi_sprite_object`. True: none is needed; the map is raw screen codes, the soldier one sprite. The maintainer's earlier run of a different brief proposed `nav_area_pathfinding`, which this game does not need either (known before this build).
3. **plan-budget, the multiplexer counted twice.** Said: sprite_multiplex_game 8,995-16,600 and sprite_slot_parking 5,334, summed. True: parking is the slot policy of the one multiplexer (the parking page says so: "the slot policy for a game multiplexer whose slot count is fixed"); both figures hold a 16-slot sort. Measured: the whole logic frame, sort, build and every IRQ included, is 3,049 cycles at most (one sprite shown, 15 parked).
4. **check-compatibility, parking against the multiplexer.** Said: `unit_contention (hard)` sprite_multiplex_game x sprite_slot_parking on `sprite_0-7` and `vic_raster_irq`, verdict INCOMPATIBLE. True: they are one multiplexer here (mux.asm); the archetype's own fingerprint names both, so every plan from the archetype starts INCOMPATIBLE.
5. **check-compatibility, an implied tile renderer.** Said: `tile_map_render` implied as a prerequisite of char_attribute_flags (through tile_grid_collision). True: char_attribute_flags reads raw screen codes (its page, "How" step 2: "tile_map_render fills such an array", an example, not a need); this map has no tiles.
6. **pitfalls-for, the scroll techniques.** Asked: `pitfalls-for threshold_scroll_v` (and row_map_redraw, soft_scroll_v). Said: 16 pitfalls, among them `fpp_write_outside_window`, `linecrunch_write_outside_window`, `mid_row_badline_write_off_by_one`, `badline_every_line_block_length`, each "Reached through: SCROLY $D011 (Register), which this technique uses". True: none applies to a one-line-a-frame YSCROLL step; the register path pulls in every `$D011` trick.
7. **pitfalls-for, techniques it cannot find.** Asked: `pitfalls-for checkpoint_respawn`. Said: "No direct entity match", then BRK and NOP patch pitfalls by text score. `pitfalls-for grenade_lob`: "No direct match for grenade_lob". True: both are techniques in the graph (`technique-lookup` finds them); they have no pitfalls, which is a different answer from "no such thing".
8. **toolchain-hint, row_map_redraw.** Asked: `toolchain-hint row_map_redraw`. Said: Oscar64 by policy, and its "canonical snippet" is the page's Variations text, no code. True: the only recipe is KickAssembler; its figures depend on a patched `LDA abs,Y` / `STA abs,Y` loop in one page (14 cycles a byte, recipe row-map-redraw). The briefing's toolchain split also leaves row_map_redraw out of the KickAssembler parts. This starter's redraw is KickAssembler (kernel.asm `redraw`).
9. **Missing: how frame_sync_loop and row_map_redraw share a frame.** row_map_redraw says start the redraw after the last playfield badline (the band's tick, line 224) and write YSCROLL in the same frame; frame_sync_loop and the multiplexer want the loop to wake after the frame IRQ applies the frame's values (line 250 here). Neither page, nor the archetype ("on a frame that runs no other game logic"), says which frame is skipped or where the loop waits. Settled here by measurement (PLAN.md, "Decisions"): the logic frame commits, waits for the band's tick, redraws; the next frame starts late (line 135 PAL, 181 NTSC) and runs no logic.
10. **Missing: parking with the game multiplexer's reject.** sprite_slot_parking parks at Y 0, where each parked slot is fetched for 21 lines (105 cycles of DMA a frame on PAL, its page); the page does not say that with `sprite_multiplex_game`'s build, which stops at the first sorted Y past its cut, a parking Y below the cut costs no hardware sprite and no DMA. mux.asm parks at Y 255 below MAX_SY 187; the verdict's `SHOWN 1` (one sprite built with 15 slots parked) shows the reject drops them.
11. **Missing: the band IRQ entered late.** The invalid-mode-band and row-map-redraw listings poll with `CMP $D012 / BNE` for an exact line. Chained after a multiplexer's late guard, an entry after that line would spin a frame (pitfall `raster_poll_equality_misses_under_dispatch_latency`); neither recipe mentions it. kernel.asm polls "while below the line" (the same 7 cycles a pass, the same exit cycle) and `make phases` passes at all eight phases on both models.
12. **char_attribute_flags in multicolour.** Its page measures the draw-behind holes with a hires checker glyph. In multicolour text the `%01` pixels are background for priority too (techniques/sprite.md, mob_priority, measured there), so a canopy's `$D022` pixels show the sprite; the page does not say so. FIREBASE's canopy uses it: its light-green highlights show the soldier.
13. **object_pool's Cost against a game's enemies.** Asked: `technique-lookup object_pool` and `wave_director`. Said: 380 cycles a frame for eight live slots (object_pool), 1,170-3,188 for wave_director with the pool inside. True: the 380 is an update that adds a velocity per slot; enemies that aim, walk against `A_BLOCK`, fire and cull cost about 600 cycles per object per thinking frame in Oscar64 C. Measured: VICE monitor `prof`, `objects_update` 3,118 cycles a call at 4.7 objects alive; after thinking on alternate frames, 2,288 mean and 4,221 worst over 200 frames (PAL, `make enemies`). The page does not say what behaviour costs: 380 / 8 is 48 cycles a slot against about 490 here with half the pool thinking each frame (2,288 / 4.7, arithmetic), ten times more.
14. **row_map_redraw with sprites on screen.** Asked: `technique-lookup row_map_redraw` (and its recipe). Said: NTSC lead 29 lines; the redraw frame keeps about 1,780 cycles on NTSC; the recipe runs with no sprites. True: sprite DMA on the lines the copy runs over comes off the lead, and the page does not say so. Measured here with eight sprites up: the redraw took 15,231 cycles instead of 14,317 on NTSC and ended on line 195 instead of 181, lead 13 lines instead of 27 (PAL 14,506 against 14,100, lead 65 against 73), `make shot check`.
15. **Objects on the redraw frame.** Asked: `technique-lookup threshold_scroll_v`, `row_map_redraw`, the archetype. Said: Commando moves its objects with the ground on the redraw frame and skips its sort there; row_map_redraw "How" step 6: run no other logic, "or only what fits". True: nothing says what that move costs or when it fits. Measured: the pool's move plus a table build without a sort is 2,553 cycles (about 39 lines, PAL). It fits on PAL (that frame ends on line 207-210). On NTSC the redraw ends on line 195 and the band IRQ holds 211-224, so it does not fit: forced, the run lost 6 frames. FIREBASE skips it after line 192 (main.c `LFX_LAST`).
16. **Missing: aimed enemy fire in eight directions.** Asked: `search "enemy bullet aimed at player direction from dx dy octant velocity table"`. Said: atan2_8bit (a byte angle, with a 16-sector compare in its Variations) and facing_turn_step. True: no page gives the 8-sector split or a slow bullet's velocity table. Used here: axis when min * 2.5 < max (tan 22.5 = 0.414, arithmetic), and velocities of 1.5 pixels a frame on an axis and 1 + 1 on a diagonal.
17. **grenade_lob, the enemy grenade and walls.** Asked: `technique-lookup grenade_lob`, `char_attribute_flags`. Said: the player's grenade ignores solid terrain; enemy bullets end on it (char_attribute_flags, Commando). Nothing is said about the enemy grenade and walls. This build makes it burst on an `A_BLOCK` cell from age 16, because the brief asked for that. Commando was not measured for it.
18. **Missing: the 16-entry bullet velocity table.** Asked: `search "player bullet one shot per press velocity table facing range"` and `technique-lookup facing_turn_step`. Said: the facing "indexes ... a 16-entry bullet velocity table"; Commando's shots are "6 px a frame straight up, 8 px a frame sideways". True: no page or recipe gives the sixteen values; the facing-turn-step recipe's `dxtab`/`dytab` are the eight-way walk steps. weapons.c derives round(20 sin a), -round(20 cos a) in quarter pixels (5 pixels a frame, 4.9 on the in-between facings, arithmetic); `make weapons` measured facings 0, 4, 6 and 12 leaving at (0, -20), (20, 0), (14, 14), (-20, 0).
19. **pitfalls-for skips a technique's own Pitfalls section.** Asked: `pitfalls-for facing_turn_step`. Said: `cia1_ddr_cleared_kills_keyboard`, `colour_ram_index_past_last_cell_hits_cia1` and `joystick2_scan_phantom_press`, each "Reached through: DC00". True: the page's own four pitfalls (a centred stick must hold the facing; up+down is not a direction; a shot during a turn takes the in-between facing; fire on the level autofires, use `joystick_edge_detect`) are the ones that apply, and the tool returns none of them. The same for `grenade_lob` (gap 7 says it has no pitfalls; its page lists four: 8-bit bounds, testing the blast once, the edges, X past 255).
20. **row_map_redraw's lead leaves out sprite DMA (the gap in 14, found apart by the weapons branch).** Asked: `technique-lookup row_map_redraw` and its page's Cycle budget. Said: leads of 75 (PAL) and 29 (NTSC) lines, interrupts named as what eats them. True: those are with no sprites on the playfield; each sprite on the lines the copy runs through takes its DMA from the copy. FIREBASE measured the NTSC lead at 27 lines with the soldier alone and 21 with up to four weapon sprites more, the redraw 14,317 then 14,662 cycles (`make weapons`, verdict row 6).
21. **Missing: Oscar64 reorders a volatile CIA store and load.** Asked: `search "Oscar64 volatile CIA store load reordered keyboard scan"`, and the root CLAUDE.md's Oscar64 gotchas. Said: keyboard_matrix_scan's "How" writes `$DC00` then reads `$DC01`, as C would; no gotcha or page says Oscar64 may move a `cia1.prb` load past a later `cia1.pra` store. True: with `port_read` (`j = cia1.pra; cia1.pra = 0x7f; if (!(cia1.prb & 0x10)) ...; cia1.pra = 0xff;`) inlined into main's loop at -O2, the local build (1.32.271 + c1270bc) emitted `LDA $DC00`, `STA $DC00` #$7F, `STA $DC00` #$FF, `LDA $DC01` (build/run-and-gun.asm at $2049-$2058), so SPACE never threw; the same function alone in a file compiles in order at -O1, -O2 and -O3. Not tried on upstream or v1.32.273; not minimised further. Fix used: the scan in `__asm`, order checked in the listing. Belongs in the Oscar64 gotchas and keyboard_matrix_scan's C form.
22. **drive.py does not say what $DC00 bits 5-7 read.** Asked: harness/drive.py's docstring. Said: port 2 is pressed through VICE's Joyport I/O simulation device on the real `$DC00`; nothing about bits 5-7. True: with that device `$DC00` read `$1F` idle (measured through drive.py's `Vice` class, `port_j` `$1F`). The KB was right and FIREBASE was wrong: techniques/input.md (joystick_edge_detect) says to mask bits 5 to 7; weapons folded the throw key into bit 5 of the unmasked byte, so under drive.py the throw read as held for ever and never fired. Fix used: `ORA #$E0` before the fold. The gap is only the harness docstring, where a game author testing with `make drive` would look.
23. **plan-budget and technique-lookup, sfx_voice_takeover's cost.** Asked: `technique-lookup sfx_voice_takeover`, and `plan-budget` on the fingerprint. Said: `cycles_per_frame=342, cycles_per_frame_typical=66`, summed as the audio's share of a frame. True: those are the effect engine's increments over a music-only frame (the recipe's own "by difference" figures); the driver's whole step, tune included, is 235-777 cycles here (recipe: 215-864), and the archetype's fingerprint has no music-player technique (`sid_play_routine_pattern` only comes in as an implied prerequisite), so no plan from the archetype counts the tune. Measured: `make audio` and verdict row 3, CIA1 timer A around each step.
24. **pitfalls-for sfx_voice_takeover, NTSC.** Asked: `pitfalls-for sfx_voice_takeover`. Said: four SID pitfalls reached through registers. True: its recipe is `region: both`, plays PAL register values (3.8 % sharp on NTSC, it says) and one tick count a frame (19 % fast on NTSC, which it does not say); `pal_ntsc_tempo_mismatch` applies and is not listed. Fixed here with an NTSC table and one tick count skipped in six; `make audio`'s tempo check passes on both models.
25. **Missing: a per-frame player beside row_map_redraw.** The redraw page and this plan say every IRQ cycle during the copy comes off its lead; no page says how to keep a once-a-frame player out of it. Done here: a hold flag set around the redraw; the frame IRQ inside it counts the step (15 cycles) and the next frame IRQ plays it first. NTSC lead 27 lines before and after (verdict row 6). Playing the owed step at the redraw's end instead pushed the light frame's end from NTSC line 205 to 232 and ran the step among badlines (868 cycles against 782).
26. **sfx-voice-takeover recipe, pattern space.** The recipe reads every voice's pattern through one table indexed by Y (`patdata,y`), so the three patterns share 255 bytes; the page does not say so. This tune's patterns are 321 bytes; the driver here patches a table address per voice at each new note.
27. **Harness, meter_init masks interrupts for four frames.** `templates/_harness/meter/frame_meter.c` calibrates with `sei` and four waits for line 0; a frame IRQ lost there is a music step lost (trace: two steps missing at the start of play in every AUTOPILOT build). Neither the harness CLAUDE.md nor the meter's header says it. Also: Oscar64's start-up copies the KickAssembler blob into place, so a store trace of the blob's addresses sees that copy first (three false "steps" here), and `watch.py`'s SID count includes it (the `NO_PLAYER` build shows 2 frames, the docstring names init and effect starts).
